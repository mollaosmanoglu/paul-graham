### Definition

<Note kind="essay" source="Startup = Growth" href="http://www.paulgraham.com/growth.html" date="2012-09">
A startup is a company designed to grow fast Being newly founded does not in itself make a company a startup.
</Note>

<Note kind="essay" source="Startup = Growth" href="http://www.paulgraham.com/growth.html" date="2012-09">
The only essential thing is growth Everything else we associate with startups follows from growth.
</Note>

### Compass

<Note kind="essay" source="Startup = Growth" href="http://www.paulgraham.com/growth.html" date="2012-09">
If you get growth, everything else tends to fall into place.
</Note>

<Note kind="essay" source="Startup = Growth" href="http://www.paulgraham.com/growth.html" date="2012-09">
You can use growth like a compass to make almost every decision you face.
</Note>

### Default alive

<Note kind="essay" source="Default Alive or Default Dead?" href="http://www.paulgraham.com/aord.html" date="2015-10">
Half the founders I talk to don't know whether they're default alive or default dead.
</Note>

<Note kind="essay" source="Default Alive or Default Dead?" href="http://www.paulgraham.com/aord.html" date="2015-10">
Instead of starting to ask too late whether you're default alive or default dead, start asking too early.
</Note>

### Notes

<Note kind="tweet" source="@paulg" href="https://x.com/paulg/status/407284043097456640" date="2013-12">
Startups that graph their growth rate (e.g. Coinbase, Zenefits) do well.  Only the toughest can face graphs where flat is good.
</Note>

<Note kind="tweet" source="@paulg" href="https://x.com/paulg/status/630132481732120576" date="2015-08">
The best way to increase a startup's growth rate is to make the product so good people recommend it to their friends.
</Note>

<Note kind="tweet" source="@paulg" href="https://x.com/paulg/status/1510175695439564800" date="2022-04">
Though startup investors are all looking for a high growth rate, growth rate is (whether they realize it or not) merely a proxy for something else: how big the company will ultimately get.
</Note>

<Note kind="tweet" source="@paulg" href="https://x.com/paulg/status/1546476751324332032" date="2022-07">
Founders who are doing well and those who are doing badly both underestimate the importance of growth rate.
</Note>

<Note kind="tweet" source="@paulg" href="https://x.com/paulg/status/1869015469342257166" date="2024-12">
AI startups seem to grow faster than previous startups. But this is just the latest stage in a consistent trend over the last 50 years. Each decade's startups grow faster than the previous decade's.
</Note>

<Note kind="tweet" source="@paulg" href="https://x.com/paulg/status/2034756891818004629" date="2026-03">
If you really want to hold yourself to a high standard, graph the growth rate of the number you care about instead of the number itself. Then you're winning if you can even keep it flat.
</Note>
