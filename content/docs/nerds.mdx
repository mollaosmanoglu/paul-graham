### Popularity

<Note kind="essay" source="Why Nerds are Unpopular" href="http://www.paulgraham.com/nerds.html" date="2003-02">
There is a strong correlation between being smart and being a nerd, and an even stronger inverse correlation between being a nerd and being popular.
</Note>

<Note kind="essay" source="Why Nerds are Unpopular" href="http://www.paulgraham.com/nerds.html" date="2003-02">
Being smart seems to make you unpopular.
</Note>

<Note kind="essay" source="Why Nerds are Unpopular" href="http://www.paulgraham.com/nerds.html" date="2003-02">
They want to be popular, certainly, but they want even more to be smart.
</Note>

<Note kind="tweet" source="@paulg" href="https://x.com/paulg/status/2051293261847793839" date="2026-05">

</Note>

<Note kind="essay" source="Fierce Nerds" href="http://www.paulgraham.com/fn.html" date="2021-05">

</Note>

<Note kind="essay" source="Fierce Nerds" href="http://www.paulgraham.com/fn.html" date="2021-05">
