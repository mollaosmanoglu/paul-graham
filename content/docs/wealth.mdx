<Note kind="tweet" source="@paulg" href="https://x.com/paulg/status/1646465300144041989" date="2023-04">
The amount of equity you'll get in a startup decreases exponentially as your employee number increases. If you want to get rich from a startup, you have to be there early, either as a founder or an early employee.
</Note>

<Note kind="tweet" source="@paulg" href="https://x.com/paulg/status/1768979667963220174" date="2024-03">
The idea that you can get rich without doing a lot of work is so false that you can use its falsity as a heuristic. You can make more by deliberately chasing hard problems.
</Note>

<Note kind="tweet" source="@paulg" href="https://x.com/paulg/status/1776645539938906613" date="2024-04">
To get rich, do work where you get paid in proportion to the number of customers you satisfy instead of the number of hours you work. Not all work of this kind will make you rich, but nearly all work that can make you rich is of this kind.
</Note>

<Note kind="tweet" source="@paulg" href="https://x.com/paulg/status/1888939980719845780" date="2025-02">
Being bitter about wealth is a good way to ensure you'll never become rich. Why would you do anything to bring you closer to something you despise?
</Note>
